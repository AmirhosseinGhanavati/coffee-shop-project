-Our Story

A passion brewed in every handcrafted cup.

Founded in 2014, Ember & Brew began as a tiny corner shop with a single espresso machine and a big dream — to bring the world's finest beans to our neighborhood.

Today, we source directly from sustainable farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Indonesia. Every bean is roasted in-house in small batches, ensuring peak flavor and a story you can taste. We believe coffee is more than a drink — it's a ritual, a pause, a moment of connection.
